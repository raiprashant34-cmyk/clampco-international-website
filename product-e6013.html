CLAMPCO · E6013

General-Purpose Welding Electrode

CLAMPCO E6013 is a rutile-type covered electrode class for carbon and mild-steel fabrication, maintenance and repair applications. It is designed for practical shop-floor welding where smooth operation and an easy-to-use general-purpose electrode are required.
