Welding Electrodes / Technical Data Sheet

CLAMPCO · E7016

Low-Hydrogen Electrode for Structural Welding

CLAMPCO E7016 is a basic, hydrogen-controlled electrode class for carbon and medium/high-tensile steel fabrication, including applications where controlled hydrogen and reliable root-pass performance are important.

Technical data

ClassificationAWS/SFA A5.1 — E7016
Electrode typeBasic / hydrogen-controlled covered electrode class.
Welding positionAll-position classification; confirm the selected product's qualified positions.
Current / polarityAC and DC variants are available in the market; final polarity must follow the supplied CLAMPCO product specification and welding procedure.
Typical available sizes2.5 mm · 3.2 mm · 4.0 mm · 5.0 mm. Other sizes subject to enquiry.
Reference operating rangesTypical commercial ranges vary by diameter; examples in published E7016 data include approximately 60–100 A for 2.5 mm, 90–130 A for 3.2 mm and 130–180 A for 4.0 mm. Final CLAMPCO values must be confirmed against the supplied specification.
Mechanical referenceAWS E7016 minimum requirements are commonly specified at about 400 MPa yield strength, 490 MPa tensile strength and 22% elongation; exact batch values depend on the supplied product.
Storage / re-dryingHydrogen-controlled electrodes require controlled storage and handling. Follow the final CLAMPCO storage and re-drying instruction supplied with the product.
ApplicationsStructural fabrication, medium/high-tensile steel work, root-pass welding, machinery, pressure-related fabrication and repair subject to procedure.

Technical note: The classification and reference values above are selection guidance, not a certificate of conformity for a particular batch. Exact chemistry, mechanical properties, diffusible hydrogen, dimensions, current range, packaging, approvals and certificates must be confirmed on the final CLAMPCO technical datasheet or quotation.
